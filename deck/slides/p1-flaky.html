Stale taps after a quiet refresh, one command at a time. Not always, which is worse than always: a sleep makes it pass on a slow day and fail on a slower one.

Each number is the black-box row of docs/checks/graybox.sh on that device, the last run before this deck: ten taps right after a quiet refresh, launched the ordinary way, each graded by the app. Over fifty more on the iPhone earlier in the day, 35 of 50 were stale. The rate depends on how fast the tool is against how long the work takes, which is exactly why a timeout tuned on one machine fails on another.
