Before every touch, three things in order

1  the lift

the last tap's finger-up has been heard

2  150 ms of grace

for work that tap starts to be announced

3  busy=0

nothing in flight, then find the target

0–15 ms

from the lift to the app's busy line, 200 trials

~35 ms

what a wait costs when the app is idle

The wait runs before the target is resolved, not just before the touch, so the target is found on the screen the work produced. The grace is ten times the worst gap measured: in 200 trials on the iPhone the app's busy line came 0 to 15 ms after the lift. With no lift heard during an action, as when the button is in an alert's own window, the grace counts from the action's end instead.
