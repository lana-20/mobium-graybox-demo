Watching changed the timing

The first Pixel recording showed no race at all. A full trace screenshots and reads the screen after every call, and every call waits for it.

no trace

1.4 s

full trace

5.1 s

trace, no screens

1.3 s

With the work lasting at most 1.6 s, five seconds per two taps let it finish first, every time. The demo records the screen as video and traces calls without screens.

Two taps, Refresh quietly then Row B, timed on the Pixel 8 Pro: 1.59 and 1.35 s untraced, 5.12 and 5.42 s with a full trace, 1.35 and 1.30 s with trace --no-screenshots --no-maps. The bars are drawn to that scale, at 200 px a second. The first Pixel run, with full traces, landed current five times in five. Mobium's trace help now says so. The general lesson: any instrument that adds time to each step can make a race disappear.
