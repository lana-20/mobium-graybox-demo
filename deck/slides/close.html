Ask  ·  wait  ·  prove

Don't guess when the app is done. Ask it, wait for its word, and make the test say what it waited for.

The screen stays the default, and needs nothing from the app. For the work a screen hides, two calls in the app and one key in the test turn a flaky tap into a steady one.

The tool, and the app the demo ran against:

Read the conclusion close to verbatim, then stop. Both repos are public and MIT licensed; the guide in the footer walks the same steps with a tutorial. Q&A material: what counts as work is the app's call; native and Flutter apps write the same lines; calling into the app, not just waiting on it, is measured but not built.
