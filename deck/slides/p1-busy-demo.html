Work the screen does not show

Refresh quietly starts 0.4 to 1.6 s of work and leaves the old rows up.

Then a new generation of rows replaces them.

Tap a row, and it says whether it was current or stale.

The app reports its own outcome, so no tool can grade itself.

iPhone 15 Plus

MobiumApp is Mobium's own app under test: every screen is a control for a failure, and the app says what happened rather than leaving the tool to infer it. The work's length is random on purpose: that is what makes a race flaky instead of reliably broken. The screenshot is the iPhone after a stale tap: the outcome line reads row B, generation 1: stale.
