No server in the app, and no port opened on anyone's phone. The way out is the device log, which a test already reads.

1 to 4 ms: on the iPhone 15 Plus, from the app writing a line to Mobium reading it over syslog_relay, with the phone's clock read through lockdown before and after the run to take out the offset (it was 37 to 38 ms ahead of the Mac). Android reads logcat from the device's own time at launch, so a line from an earlier launch is never read, and a restart picks up from the last line's time, so nothing in a gap is lost.
