Auto-wait is right about the screen. The screen is wrong about the app. No amount of looking harder fixes that.

These are Mobium's actionability checks, the ones every action makes before it touches anything (docs/guides/autowait.md). A spinner would fail visible on the rows, and Mobium would wait it out: that is the control in the demo, Refresh with a spinner, current every time. The quiet refresh leaves nothing on screen to wait for.
