Busy is a lease, not a promise

While work is in flight the app restates it every half second, from a native timer. Unrenewed for 1.5 s, it is stale: the app crashed holding it, or was suspended.

Crash during a refresh, then a tap  ·  emulator

$ mobium tap 300 30

tapped (300, 30)

gray box: not waited — the app stopped saying it is busy 3s ago (it was busy with doomed)

Without the lease, that tap would have waited 10 s for a dead app, then failed.

The renewal runs on a native timer, not on the JavaScript thread, so an app that is busy in JavaScript still renews, and a dead or suspended process stops. The output is verbatim from the Pixel 7 emulator run's transcript: Crash during a refresh holds a busy lease named doomed and dies 300 ms in; the next tap, on the status bar, is not held up. On Android the app often says away on its way down, and then that is the reason given: the same answer, sooner.
